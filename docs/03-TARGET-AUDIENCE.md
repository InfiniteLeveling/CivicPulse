# Target audience and adoption hypotheses

These personas guide design and recruitment. No interviews or purchasing commitments have been verified. Permissions derive from the account role and assignment, never from demographic characteristics.

| Persona and stage | Objective and current frustration to investigate | Device and skill assumption | Accessibility and adoption barrier | Features and allowed actions | Expected benefit to test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Citizen · launch | Report a leak once and find the response without calling | Smartphone, familiar with photos, unfamiliar with map coordinates | Plain language and large controls; distrust of silent queues | Citizen creates, supports, tracks, verifies own report | Can locate next action unaided |
| Student · launch | Report a broken light on the walk to class; uncertain which office handles it | Phone camera, intermittent campus Wi-Fi | One-hand operation and manual pin; login friction | Same citizen role, optional suggestions and nearby support | Less time spent identifying a contact |
| Elderly resident · future community pilot | Follow a repair without revisiting an office | Phone or tablet, may prefer guided steps | Zoom, keyboard, list and readable labels; email confirmation may require help | Citizen, assisted intake with consent, own timeline | Can inspect status independently |
| Municipal administrator · future buyer representative | Allocate work and justify decisions across departments | Desktop, accustomed to queues and spreadsheets | Searchable tables and non-color severity labels; procurement and jurisdiction | Admin in a separately agreed deployment; assign, moderate and view aggregates | Can audit ownership and time to action |
| Department officer · launch or simulated | Find assigned cases and avoid stale dispatch instructions | Laptop at desk, phone on rounds | Short forms, clear conflicts; unclear escalation authority | Officer reads and transitions assigned department cases | Fewer unowned handoffs |
| Maintenance worker · future dedicated app | Find the correct fixture and document repair on site | Phone, variable connectivity and gloves | Large targets, short notes; unreliable data coverage | Pilot work relayed by officer; future delegated work-order role requires policy | Fewer repeat site visits |
| Residential society administrator · future buyer | Assign shared-area maintenance and answer resident queries | Desktop and phone, moderate technical skill | Simple filters; resident privacy and building boundaries | Potential site admin after tenant isolation exists | Can demonstrate repair follow-through |
| NGO coordinator · future collaborator | Identify recurring neighborhood problems without exposing residents | Laptop for planning, phone in field | Accessible aggregate exports; limited authority to act | Citizen permissions initially; future consented aggregate access | Can direct outreach using defensible trends |

## Recruitment and permission model

A campus facilities sponsor could fund a pilot. Students and residents supply reports, an officer owns a queue and a separately provisioned admin handles cross-department decisions. A purchasing contact does not receive staff permissions by default. Confirm one actual operator, test the journey with people of different abilities and record permission misunderstandings before broadening access. See [pilot questions](21-OPEN-QUESTIONS.md) and [security](12-SECURITY-AND-PRIVACY.md).
