# Problem statement

A report is actionable when someone can identify the asset, locate it, decide who owns the repair and tell the reporter what happened. A photograph in a group chat may supply the first clue but omit the location or be buried before anyone takes ownership. A second report of the same leak can then become a second work order while a separate nearby leak gets mistakenly dismissed.

| Perspective | Plausible failure and cause | Consequence | How to validate in the pilot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resident | No reference after a call or forwarded message | Repeated follow-up and uncertain closure | Ask reporters to reconstruct the last issue from submission to closure |
| Maintenance desk | Different channels gather different fields | Time spent locating assets or forwarding cases | Review anonymized intake examples for location and owner |
| Field worker | Vague address or several similar fixtures | Wrong fixture repaired or unnecessary visit | Walk sample points with maintenance staff |
| Supervisor | Loudest complaint dominates, no aging or severity rationale | Persistent low-visibility problems and delayed hazard review | Compare two independently ranked queues with reasons |
| Community | Repair marked complete without shared evidence | Disputes are hard to investigate | Ask what proof both parties would accept |

These are problem hypotheses from the brief. Local complaint volumes, resolution delays, resident interviews and authority participation have **Status: Not verified / Requires confirmation**. Do not present a baseline reduction or a measured social benefit until comparable pilot observations exist.

## Intervention and limits

Collect a confirmed point, category, description and photo once. Give the author a reference, the desk an assignment queue and both parties a dated history. Suggest possible duplicates while preserving the choice to file a distinct issue. Track whether intake is more complete, handoffs are legible and repairs can be verified. Record disagreement as research evidence.

The proposed campus trial covers ordinary infrastructure maintenance. Emergency response, statutory complaint handling, verified identity, inter-agency dispatch and repair staffing need institutional agreements. A working prototype alone cannot establish who will perform repairs. See [target audience](03-TARGET-AUDIENCE.md), [risk analysis](13-RISK-ANALYSIS.md) and [open questions](21-OPEN-QUESTIONS.md).
